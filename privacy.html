Little Finn Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Little Finn Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Little Finn" YouTube channel to upload videos that the owner has created to that same channel.

What the app accesses

  • The app asks for one permission: to upload videos to the YouTube channel chosen by the signed-in user (Google scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, also written "youtube.upload").
  • It does not read, collect or store any information about viewers, other users or other channels.

How data is used and stored

  • The app sends to YouTube only the videos and their titles, descriptions and tags, chosen by the channel owner.
  • The authorization token given by Google is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared with anyone.
  • No personal data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Little Finn Uploader's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, the app uses the access it is given only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel, and does not use or transfer that access for any other purpose.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account security settings: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After access is revoked, the token stored on the computer no longer works.

Children

The "Little Finn" channel is made for small children, but the app itself is used by the adult channel owner only. The app collects no data from children or from viewers.

Contact

For any question about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/P0zzi/P0zzi.github.io/issues.
