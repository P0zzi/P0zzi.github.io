Little Finn

Short 3D cartoons for small children

Little Finn Uploader

A small private tool that helps the owner of the Little Finn YouTube channel upload new videos to that same channel.

The Little Finn channel

Little Finn is a YouTube channel of short 3D cartoons for small children: songs, short stories and simple learning games. Finn is a five-year-old boy in a shark hoodie who lives underwater, and his friends are Pip the octopus, Coco the clownfish and Toots the turtle. The episodes are short, colourful and gentle, made for little viewers.

About Little Finn Uploader

Little Finn Uploader is the name of the app that appears on the Google sign-in and consent screen. It is a private tool used only by the owner of the Little Finn channel. It takes videos that the owner has made and uploads them to the owner's own Little Finn channel on YouTube, with a title, description and tags chosen by the owner, and scheduled to be published at a chosen date and time.

It is not a public service and it has no features for viewers or for other channels.

The one permission the app uses

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – this lets the app upload videos to the YouTube channel of the signed-in user. Nothing else is requested.

The app does not read viewer data, other channels or comments. The authorization token is stored only on the owner's own computer.
